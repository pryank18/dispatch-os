The problem

Every distributor runs the same three fires.

None of them are about demand. They're about the plumbing between you and your retailers.

01 — ORDERING

Orders arrive as voice notes

Your salesman calls a shop, half-hears the order over a generator running in the background, and writes it down wrong. It ships wrong. It gets returned. You eat the cost.

02 — CREDIT

Credit lives in someone's memory

Which retailer is close to their limit? Nobody knows until a cheque bounces. Bad debt isn't a market problem — it's a visibility problem.

03 — STOCK

Stockouts you find out about too late

A fast-moving SKU runs out at the warehouse and nobody notices until three retailers have already tried to order it and given up.

04 — VISIBILITY

No single view of the business

Sales, stock, and dues sit in three different notebooks and an Excel sheet nobody updates on time. Month-end reconciliation takes days.
