# Dispatch OS — Market Requirements Document

## Market problem

FMCG distribution to small retail (kirana stores, general stores) is still run largely offline: orders by phone or voice note, credit tracked from memory, and stock visibility that lags reality. This is a large and highly fragmented market, and the operational pain — lost orders, bad debt, stockouts — scales with the number of retailers a distributor serves, yet most distributors have no dedicated system, only WhatsApp and notebooks.

## Target market

FMCG distributors and wholesalers serving a network of retail shops, particularly those currently coordinating orders manually and without an existing ERP or ordering system. The product is scoped for single- to multi-warehouse distributors (Starter through Scale tiers) rather than large enterprise distribution chains.

## Why now

Retailers increasingly have smartphone access, making a self-serve ordering channel viable even for small shops that were previously reachable only by phone. At the same time, enterprise distribution software remains priced and scoped for large multi-city operators, leaving a gap for a lightweight platform built for a single distributor running one or a few warehouses.

## Competitive landscape

- **Enterprise distribution/ERP platforms** — comprehensive but priced and scoped for large, multi-city distribution networks
- **Generic order-taking apps / WhatsApp Business** — lower friction to adopt but no built-in credit enforcement, stock visibility, or dashboard
- **Status quo (phone, voice note, notebook)** — the default for most small distributors today; zero software cost but carries the ordering, credit, and visibility problems Dispatch OS is built to remove

## Value proposition

Dispatch OS gives a single distributor their own retailer-facing ordering platform — with credit limits enforced automatically and one dashboard for orders, stock, and credit exposure — at a flat monthly cost scoped to their size, rather than an enterprise ERP price tag.

## Positioning

A distributor-first ordering and credit platform for the small and mid-size distribution segment that enterprise distribution software doesn't reach — positioned as the step up from phone/WhatsApp ordering, not a competitor to large-scale ERP suites.

## Status

Live demo with a defined three-tier pricing model (Starter, Growth, Scale) ready for go-to-market validation with real distributors.
